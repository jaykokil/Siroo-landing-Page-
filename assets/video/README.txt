Put your two video files here.

  siroo-overview.mp4    -> plays from the "Watch Video" button in the hero
  siroo-in-action.mp4   -> plays from the "Watch SIROO In Action" band

Then point CONFIG.videos in assets/js/main.js at them.

You can also use a YouTube or Vimeo link instead of a file — paste the normal
URL into CONFIG.videos and the player switches to an embed automatically.
For anything longer than about 30 seconds, hosting on YouTube/Vimeo is the
better option: a 2-minute 1080p MP4 is 30-60 MB and slow on mobile data.
