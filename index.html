Draft Beer

The stock most systems can't count.

A keg can't be eyeballed, and by the time it blows you've already lost the record of what came out of it. SIROO registers each keg as its own asset — capacity, tare weight, which bars it feeds — then tracks it to closure.

At each count you enter one number: the current weight. SIROO works out what's left, what was poured, and prompts for wastage. Record sales by serve and the variance is calculated for you.
